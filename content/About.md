PAR Garden is a public thinking space built in Obsidian and published with Quartz.

## Why this exists

- To think through complex topics in public
- To connect ideas across domains
- To turn notes into reusable maps and experiments

## Core domains

- DevSecOps and platform engineering
- Mindfulness and integral practice
- Liberal education and language learning
- Energy systems and food resilience

## How to use the garden

- Use [Start Here](/start-here) for onboarding
- Use [Atlas](/Atlas) for map-style browsing
- Use [Efforts](/Efforts) for active and sleeping projects
- Use [Tags](/tags) to traverse themes

## Publishing setup

- Authoring: [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md)
- Method: [Linking Your Thinking](https://www.linkingyourthinking.com/)
- Publisher: [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz)
