#  Advisory + Product Strategy: From Fawkes Platform to Thought Leadership

_A flexible, education-focused approach leveraging your Fawkes platform and natural consultative style_

## Executive Summary

This strategy transforms your Fawkes platform and DevOps expertise into a sustainable advisory + product business that generates $50K+ Year 1, $100K+ Year 2 while building toward thought leadership. The approach mirrors your successful SAIC Dojo model: combining open source platform capabilities with educational delivery to solve real client challenges.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation & Learning (Months 1-6)

_Build advisory understanding while launching platform-driven offerings_

### 1.1 Advisory Role Education & Positioning

**Understanding Advisory Types:**

**Strategic Advisor (Recommended for You)**

- **Time**: 4-8 hours/month per client
- **Format**: Monthly strategic sessions, quarterly planning, ad-hoc problem solving
- **Compensation**: $3-8K/month retainer or equity (0.25-1%)
- **Value**: Respond to challenges, provide strategic guidance, leverage experience
- **Perfect fit**: Your consultative style and broad industry learning interest

**Fractional Executive (Optional)**

- **Time**: 1-2 days/week per client
- **Format**: Ongoing operational involvement
- **Compensation**: $8-15K/month
- **Note**: Might conflict with your travel/flexibility goals

**Board Advisor (Future Goal)**

- **Time**: 4-8 hours/quarter per company
- **Format**: Quarterly board meetings, strategic guidance
- **Compensation**: Equity-focused (0.5-2%)
- **Requirements**: Established reputation, relevant scale experience

### 1.2 Fawkes Platform Repositioning Strategy

**Platform Evolution Plan:**

- **Month 1-2**: Complete restructuring with focus on educational integration
- **Month 3-4**: Create "Fawkes Learning Labs" - hands-on environments
- **Month 5-6**: Develop assessment frameworks using platform data

**Educational Integration (SAIC Dojo Model 2.0):**

- **Interactive Learning**: Teams use Fawkes while learning DORA+ methodology
- **Real-time Metrics**: Live feedback on NPS, feature adoption, value projection
- **Cohort-based Discovery**: Groups work through actual challenges together

### 1.3 Network Activation Strategy

**Organic Growth Approach (Respects Your Style):**

- **Monthly Coffee Chats**: 4-5 reconnection calls with network contacts
- **Value-First Outreach**: Share insights about broader metrics philosophy
- **Learning-Focused**: "I'm exploring advisory work, curious about your experience"
- **No Sales Pressure**: Focus on relationship nurturing and learning

**Content Sharing Strategy:**

- **Weekly LinkedIn Posts**: Share insights from client challenges (anonymized)
- **Monthly Newsletter**: "Beyond DORA: The Full Value Chain" - 500-1000 subscribers target
- **Quarterly Case Studies**: Deep dives into platform + education success stories

## Phase 2: Product Development & Advisory Launch (Months 4-9)

_Launch core offerings while building advisory pipeline_

### 2.1 Core Product Offerings

**1. "DevOps Value Chain Assessment" ($15-25K)**

- **Duration**: 2-3 weeks, 15-20 hours total effort
- **Deliverables**: DORA+ metrics baseline, improvement roadmap, Fawkes implementation plan
- **Differentiation**: Goes beyond DORA to include NPS, feature adoption, business value metrics
- **Client Profile**: Engineering leaders wanting comprehensive view of delivery performance

**2. "Fawkes Learning Intensive" ($8-15K per cohort)**

- **Format**: 2-day workshop + 30-day implementation support
- **Participants**: 8-12 engineering leaders per session
- **Content**: Hands-on platform usage while learning metrics methodology
- **Revenue Model**: Quarterly intensives, 4 per year target

**3. "Strategic Advisory Retainer" ($5-8K/month)**

- **Services**: Monthly strategic sessions, quarterly planning, ad-hoc problem solving
- **Time Commitment**: 6-8 hours/month per client
- **Target**: 3-4 clients maximum for work-life balance
- **Focus**: Respond to challenges, provide strategic guidance

### 2.2 Advisory Client Acquisition

**Ideal Advisory Client Profile:**

- **Company Stage**: Series A/B startups or growth-stage companies (50-500 engineers)
- **Challenge**: Scaling engineering practices while maintaining quality
- **Leadership**: CTO or VP Engineering open to strategic guidance
- **Compensation**: Mix of cash retainer ($3-5K/month) + equity (0.25-0.5%)

**Acquisition Strategy:**

- **Network Warm Introductions**: Leverage your 1400 connections systematically
- **Content-Driven Leads**: Prospects find you through educational content
- **Partner Referrals**: Build relationships with VCs, executive recruiters
- **Speaking Opportunities**: Position as expert at DevOps/CTO events

### 2.3 Platform Commercialization

**Fawkes-as-a-Service Development:**

- **Community Edition**: Free, open source, drives adoption
- **Professional Edition**: $500-2000/month, enhanced analytics and support
- **Enterprise Edition**: $5-15K/month, custom integrations and advisory support

**Revenue Timeline:**

- **Months 4-6**: Complete platform restructuring
- **Months 7-9**: Launch community edition, build user base
- **Months 10-12**: Launch professional edition with paying customers

## Risk Management & Contingencies

### Market Risks

**Risk**: Economic downturn affecting advisory spending **Mitigation**: Focus on ROI-demonstrable offerings, flexible pricing models

**Risk**: Platform adoption slower than expected **Mitigation**: Strong educational component drives adoption, multiple revenue streams

### Operational Risks

**Risk**: Time management with multiple advisory clients **Mitigation**: Clear boundaries, systematic processes, selective client acceptance

**Risk**: Platform development complexity **Mitigation**: Phased approach, community feedback, focus on core value proposition

### Competitive Risks

**Risk**: Larger players entering DORA+ space **Mitigation**: Open source strategy, educational differentiation, network effects

**Risk**: Advisory market saturation **Mitigation**: Vertical specialization, unique methodology, relationship-based growth
