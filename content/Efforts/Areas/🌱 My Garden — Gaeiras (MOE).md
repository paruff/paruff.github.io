# 🌱 My Garden — Gaeiras (MOE)

## 🧭 Purpose
The garden at Gaeiras is a permanent living system — productive, resilient, and integrated with the property's energy and water resources. Permaculture principles guide decisions. Linked to [[🥗 Food Independence (MOE)]].

## 📐 Standards
> What does "good enough" look like?

- Seasonal tasks done on time (planting, pruning, harvest)
- Water use minimised — rain and greywater preferred over mains
- Soil health improving year on year
- Orchard producing fruit
- No chemical inputs

## 💧 Water and Energy Integration

- Solar surplus >4kW + battery >80% → run irrigation pump (free energy window — flagged automatically)
- Rain >10mm → skip irrigation (flagged automatically from weather data)
- Rain >15mm → check drainage
- Greywater from house → add routing details here
- Compost from kitchen waste → closed loop nutrition

## 🔗 Energy Flags (automatic)
These come from `generate_flags.py` and appear on the Dashboard:
- Rain >10mm → skip irrigation today
- Rain =0 + temp >28°C → water garden today
- Wind >40km/h → secure garden structures
- Temp >35°C → heat stress risk, mulch and shade
- Solar surplus >4kW + battery >80% → free irrigation window open
