# 🧑🏻‍🏫 EB eLearning Course (MOE)

## Project Overview

**Status**: Active - Mid Development  
**Purpose**: Establish a systematic process for creating evidence-based online training modules that can be applied to virtually any subject matter

### Goals & Objectives
- Create a repeatable, evidence-based framework for online course development
- Leverage AI tools while maintaining pedagogical integrity
- Enable efficient knowledge transfer across diverse topics
- Produce engaging, effective learning experiences

### Target Audience
- Educational content developers
- Instructional designers
- Subject matter experts who need to create training
