### Read a book prompt :

ROLE =  education tutor
TARGET = A 59 year old  retired IT professional .
FORMAT =  detailed summary
RESOURCE = As you like it by William Shakespeare 

CONTEXT:

I am a [TARGET] . I will be reading Or listening to  [RESOURCE]. I want to prepare for this reading/listening.

**ROLE**:

You are an Impartial expert [ROLE]  with 20 years of experience supporting [TARGET] audience. 

**ACTION**:

Please review the [RESOURCE] and provide the inspectional, analytical and syntropic reading based on the process outlined in the book "How to read a book"

**FORMAT:** 

For the format of this  [FORMAT].

**TARGET AUDIENCE:** 

The  audience for this is [TARGET].
