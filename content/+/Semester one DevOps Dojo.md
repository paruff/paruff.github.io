Post 1 – Welcome to the DORA Dojo (White Belt)

  

  

📊 Too many teams treat DORA like a scoreboard. Wrong move.

👉 DORA = compass, not trophy.

  

⚡ Drill:

  

- Guess your DF, LT, CFR, MTTR as a team
- Then check actuals
- Compare → where’s the biggest gap?

  

  

🧭 Compass sketch:

   N = Lead Time

W = Deploy Freq   E = MTTR

   S = CFR

💡 Challenge: Which metric feels most alive in your team right now?

  

  

  

  

Post 2 – Deployment Frequency: Repetition Kata (Yellow Belt)

  

  

🎵 Shipping is like scales in music: practice = fluency.

  

⚡ Drill: “Friday Release Dojo”

  

- Deploy 1 small change
- Practice rollback
- Reflect

  

  

Checklist:

✅ Build → ✅ Deploy → ✅ Rollback → ✅ Reflect

  

💡 Challenge: Can you add one extra deploy this week?

  

  

  

  

Post 3 – Lead Time: Flow Mapping Sparring (Yellow → Green Belt)

  

  

🚦 Lead time shows where work waits, not just works.

  

⚡ Drill:

  

- Run a 20-min value stream map with sticky notes
- Circle 1 handoff you can shorten this sprint

  

  

📉 ASCII Flow:

Idea → Backlog → Dev → Review → Deploy → User

  

💡 Challenge: Where does your work wait the longest?

  

  

  

  

Post 4 – Change Failure Rate: Safe-to-Fail Experiments (Green Belt)

  

  

🔥 Failure rate isn’t about blame — it’s about learning risk.

  

⚡ Drill:

  

- Run a canary deploy at 10% traffic
- Watch → rollback if needed
- Debrief

  

  

📋 Safe Change Kata:

Small → Observable → Reversible

  

💡 Challenge: How do you test changes safely today?

Post 6 – Balance of the Metrics (Blue → Purple Belt)

  

  

⚖️ Optimize one metric too hard → another breaks.

DF ↑ too much? CFR might spike.

  

⚡ Drill:

  

- Plot your 4 metrics on a 2x2
- Discuss trade-offs as a team

  

  

ASCII sketch:

 DF ↑        LT ↓

 CFR ↓       MTTR ↓

💡 Challenge: Which metric are you over-rotating on?

  

  

  

  

Post 7 – Feedback Dojos (Purple Belt)

  

  

📢 Metrics without feedback = noise.

  

⚡ Drill: Run a retro with just 3 Qs:

  

1. What did this metric teach us?
2. What’s our next small experiment?
3. What will we stop tracking?

  

  

💡 Challenge: When’s the last time a retro was metric-focused?

  

  

  

  

Post 8 – CI/CD: Your Dojo Floor (Purple Belt)

  

  

⚡ Every commit = a rep. Pipelines are practice mats.

  

⚡ Drill:

  

- Measure your longest CI/CD step
- Shave 10% off this sprint

  

  

Pipeline Kata:

Trigger → Build → Test → Deploy

  

💡 Challenge: What slows your pipeline most?

  

  

  

  

Post 9 – Incident Learning = Sparring (Brown Belt)

  

  

🥊 Incidents = sparring matches. Blameless = progress.

  

⚡ Drill:

  

- Run a roleplay postmortem
- Assign neutral observers
- Capture learnings, not blame

  

  

🛠️ Tool: 5 Whys template

💡 Challenge: Try a roleplay postmortem this month.

  

  

  

  

Post 10 – Coaching with Metrics (Brown Belt)

  

  

👩‍🏫 Leaders = senseis. Don’t demand numbers → guide practice.

  

⚡ Drill: In your next 1:1, ask:

👉 “What did this metric teach you?”

  

💡 Challenge: Leaders, try this Q in your next retro.

  

  

  

  

Post 11 – Platform Reliability Dojo (Black Belt)

  

  

🏯 Reliability isn’t assumed. It’s trained.

  

⚡ Drill: Run a resilience game day

  

- Pick 1 failure mode
- Simulate + recover
- Debrief

  

  

ASCII Pillars:

| Observability | Resilience | Recovery |

💡 Challenge: When’s your next game day scheduled?

  

  

  

  

Post 12 – Graduation: Continuous Learning (Black Belt)

  

  

🎓 Dojo practice never ends. The real mastery = building your own kata.

  

⚡ Drill: Write a Team Dojo Charter:

  

- Our core metric compass
- Our practice kata
- Our feedback ritual

  

  

💡 Challenge: What’s the first kata your team will adopt?
