I'll provide detailed routines focusing on pre-game, post-game, and daily conditioning exercises.

Pre-Game Warm-Up (15-20 minutes):
1. Dynamic Mobility (5-7 minutes)
- Leg swings (forward/side): 10 each leg
- Walking lunges with rotation: 10 steps
- Knee circles: 10 clockwise, 10 counterclockwise each leg
- Ankle rolls: 10 each direction

2. Activation Exercises (5-7 minutes)
- Body weight squats: 2 sets of 10 
- Lateral band walks: 2 sets of 10 steps each direction
- Glute bridges: 2 sets of 12 
- Mini-squats with resistance band: 2 sets of 10

Post-Game Recovery (15-20 minutes):
1. Static Stretching
- Quadriceps stretch: 30 seconds each leg
- Hamstring stretch: 30 seconds each leg
- Calf stretches: 30 seconds each leg
- IT band stretch: 30 seconds each side

2. Cool Down
- Foam rolling (5-7 minutes)
  * Quadriceps
  * IT band
  * Calves

Daily Strength & Balance Routine (30-40 minutes):

Core Strengthening:
1. Planks
- Standard plank: 3 sets, hold 30-45 seconds
- Side planks: 3 sets, 30 seconds each side
- Plank with leg lifts: 2 sets, 10 repetitions

2. Stability Exercises
- Bird dog: 3 sets, 10 repetitions each side
- Dead bug: 3 sets, 10 repetitions
- Swiss ball core rotations: 2 sets, 12 repetitions

Quadriceps & Knee Stability:
1. Strength Exercises
- Wall squats: 3 sets, hold 30-45 seconds
- Step-ups: 3 sets, 12 repetitions each leg
- Single leg squats: 3 sets, 8-10 repetitions each leg
- Leg press (if gym access): 3 sets, 12 repetitions

2. Balance Work
- Single leg balance: 3 sets, 30 seconds each leg
- Bosu ball squats: 2 sets, 10 repetitions
- Stability disc single leg stands: 2 sets, 30 seconds each leg

Flexibility & Mobility:
- Yoga or stretching routine: 2-3 times per week
- Focus on hip openers, hamstring flexibility

Progression:
- Start with bodyweight exercises
- Add resistance bands/light weights after 2-3 weeks
- Increase repetitions/hold times as strength improves

Recovery Considerations:
- Rest days between intense workouts
- Listen to your body
- Use ice/heat therapy as needed
- Stay hydrated
- Proper nutrition

Additional Recommendations:
- Consult physiotherapist for personalized assessment
- Consider gait analysis
- Regular sports massage

Yoga Routine for Core Strength, Balance, and Knee Support (45-60 minutes):

Warm-Up (10 minutes):
1. Cat-Cow Poses (5 minutes)
- Spinal mobility
- Core engagement
- Gentle warm-up for entire body

2. Sun Salutations A & B (5 minutes)
- Dynamic movement
- Full-body activation
- Improves circulation and flexibility

Strength and Stability Sequence (20-25 minutes):
1. Warrior Series (10 minutes)
- Warrior I, II, III poses
- Benefits:
  * Quadriceps strengthening
  * Balance improvement
  * Knee stabilization
  * Core engagement

2. Balance Poses (10-15 minutes)
- Tree Pose (Vrksasana)
  * Single-leg balance
  * Improves proprioception
- Eagle Pose (Garudasana)
  * Knee and ankle stability
  * Core strength
- Half Moon Pose (Ardha Chandrasana)
  * Advanced balance
  * Hip and knee strength

Core and Flexibility (10-15 minutes):
1. Plank Variations
- Standard Plank
- Side Planks
- Plank with leg lifts

2. Core-Specific Poses
- Boat Pose (Navasana)
- Boat Pose variations
- Bicycle Crunches

Knee-Specific Considerations:
Modifications for Existing Knee Issues:
- Use props (blocks, straps)
- Avoid deep knee bends
- Focus on alignment
- Use wall support for balance poses

Comparative Analysis:

Yoga Routine Advantages:
+ Holistic approach to strength and flexibility
+ Mindfulness and stress reduction
+ Improved body awareness
+ Low-impact exercise
+ Enhances joint mobility
+ Promotes better breathing

Potential Limitations:
- Less targeted muscle building compared to weight training
- May require more time to build pure strength
- Requires proper technique to avoid injury

Recommendation:
Hybrid Approach:
1. 2-3 yoga sessions per week
2. 1-2 targeted strength training sessions
3. Consult a yoga instructor specializing in sports/rehabilitation

Complementary Practices:
- Yin Yoga for deep stretching
- Restorative Yoga for recovery
- Meditation for mental focus

Progression Strategy:
- Start with beginner modifications
- Gradually increase pose complexity
- Listen to your body
- Work with a qualified yoga instructor
