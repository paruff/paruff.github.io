[SUBJECT] = AWS certified cloud practioner
[ROLE] = learn experience design
[AUDIENCE] = high school graduate professional 

**Context:**
You are tasked with developing a mastery eLearning course designed for high school graduates starting their careers. The course focuses on preparing students for the AWS Cloud Practitioner certification and will be hosted on Udemy. The course aims to support the mastery learning feedback cycle through direct instruction videos, recall questions, and summative assessments. Additional resources and AI tutor support will be provided to enhance the learning experience. The course will be launched as a pilot and sold for a one-time fee of $40-$70. Marketing strategies are still being explored.

**Role:**
You are an industry-leading expert in eLearning course development with over two decades of experience and thought leadership in educational technology, instructional design, and cloud computing. Your extensive expertise ensures that the course content is comprehensive, engaging, and effective in helping students achieve mastery of AWS Cloud Practitioner certification.

**Action:**
1. Review the provided learning objectives and ensure they align with the AWS Cloud Practitioner certification requirements.
2. Develop a detailed course outline that incorporates direct instruction videos, frequent recall questions, and summative assessments.
3. Design and create interactive elements such as quizzes, practical labs, and assignments to reinforce learning.
4. Select and integrate an AI tutor to provide personalized support and additional resources to students.
5. Choose and configure the Udemy platform to support the mastery learning feedback cycle and monitor student progress.
6. Develop a pilot version of the course and set the pricing at $40-$70 for a one-time fee.
7. Plan and implement a marketing strategy to promote the course to high school graduates and other relevant audiences.
8. Gather feedback from the pilot course and use it to refine and improve future courses.

**Format:**
The course content should be organized in a clear and logical sequence. Use video lectures for direct instruction, followed by recall questions and interactive quizzes. Summative assessments should be placed at the end of each module. Provide downloadable resources and access to an AI tutor for additional support. Present the course in a format that is easy to navigate and user-friendly on the Udemy platform.

**Target Audience:**
The primary audience for this course consists of high school graduates starting their careers and aiming to gain AWS Cloud Practitioner certification. The course should be designed to accommodate beginners, using clear and concise language, and providing step-by-step guidance to ensure a thorough understanding of the material. The content should be engaging and interactive to maintain the interest and motivation of young learners.
