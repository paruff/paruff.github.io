# FCPL Reciprocal Library Card System

> [!info] Key Rule
> Persons who live, work, own property, or **attend school** in reciprocal jurisdictions are entitled to a free Fairfax County Public Library card. Reciprocal borrowers get all FCPL privileges **except Interlibrary Loans**.

> [!warning] Fairfax Kanopy Status
> FCPL ended its Kanopy subscription January 31, 2025 due to costs nearly doubling. They are "on the lookout for similar services with more sustainable pricing." Use a **Montgomery County or Prince William card** for Kanopy access in the meantime.

> [!tip] Great Courses via Kanopy
> The Great Courses lecture series is available through **Kanopy** at libraries that still carry it (Montgomery, Prince William, Alexandria, etc.). It is NOT a separate subscription — it's bundled inside Kanopy.

## FCPL LinkedIn Learning Access

**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/learning-login/go/fairfaxlibrary

**Org ID if prompted:** `fairfaxlibrary`

Requirements:
- Active FCPL card (must have been used within the past year)
- 14-digit card number + PIN

> [!tip]
> Reciprocal borrowers fully qualify. If your card has lapsed, you can reset it at any FCPL branch or online.
