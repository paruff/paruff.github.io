# 🔮 Integral Life Practice (MOE)

## 🧭 Purpose
A structured approach to whole-life development based on Ken Wilber's Integral framework. ILP treats the cultivation of body, mind, soul, and spirit — across individual and collective dimensions — as a single integrated practice rather than separate pursuits.

> "The most important thing is to engage in an ILP that touches all the essential modules — body, mind, spirit, shadow — in an integral way." — Ken Wilber

## 🗺 The AQAL Framework
> All Quadrants, All Levels, All Lines, All States, All Types

### Four Quadrants
| Quadrant | Domain | Perspective | Key Practice |
|----------|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Upper Left (UL) | Mind / consciousness | I — interior individual | Meditation, shadow work, psychotherapy |
| Upper Right (UR) | Body / behaviour | It — exterior individual | Exercise, diet, sleep, physiology |
| Lower Left (LL) | Culture / relationships | We — interior collective | Relationships, ethics, worldview |
| Lower Right (LR) | Systems / society | Its — exterior collective | Work, environment, finances, community |

### 🌑 Gold: Shadow — 3-2-1 Process
The most neglected module. Shadow work integrates disowned aspects of self.

**The 3-2-1 Process:**
1. **Face it** (3rd person) — describe the shadow figure or quality: "It is..."
2. **Talk to it** (2nd person) — dialogue: "You are... I feel..."
3. **Be it** (1st person) — embody: "I am..."

**Triggers for shadow work:**
- Strong emotional reaction to another person
- Persistent negative pattern
- Dream figures that disturb you
- Qualities you strongly admire or strongly despise in others

**Your practice:**
