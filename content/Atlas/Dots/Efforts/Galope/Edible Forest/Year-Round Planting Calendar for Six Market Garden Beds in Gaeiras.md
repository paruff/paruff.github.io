## Year-Round Planting Calendar for Six Market Garden Beds in Gaeiras

### Legend:
- P: Planting
- M: Maintenance/Growing Period
- H: Main Harvest Period
- →: Continued from previous month
- ←: Continues into next month

### Additional Notes:

1. **Weather Adaptation**: Adjust planting and harvesting dates by 1-2 weeks based on current weather conditions. During unusually cold winters, use row covers to protect winter crops.

2. **Water Conservation**: The Silver Coast's dry summers (particularly July-August) require consistent irrigation. Install drip irrigation system for Beds 5 and 6 where water needs are highest.

3. **Succession Planting**: Many beds show multiple plantings of the same crop throughout the year - this maximizes production by immediately replanting after harvest.

4. **Protected Cultivation**: Early plantings of heat-loving crops (tomatoes, cucumbers) benefit from temporary low tunnels or cloches until nighttime temperatures consistently stay above 10°C.

5. **Cover Crops**: Used strategically to build soil fertility and prevent erosion during transition periods.
