# Effort Shutdown

> [!check] Shutdown Ritual
> - Write “Last” (what you just did)
> - Write “Next” (the next tiny step)
> - Reset the micro‑zone (10–20 seconds)
> - Return the physical marker to its place
> - Add one line to Daily Anchor
> - Close the Effort page with a sense of completion

> [!note] Evidence Base
> - Writing “next step” reduces restart friction (Zeigarnik effect)
> - Micro‑resetting preserves clarity (environmental cue theory)
> - Brief reflection improves retention (cognitive consolidation)
